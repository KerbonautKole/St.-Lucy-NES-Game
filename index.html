Controls: Arrow Keys, Z to interact, Enter to Pause/Continue | If you get stuck on a level, press Enter then select Restart to reset the level.


Hints
If a level has coins, collect them to unlock the exit.
If you step on a cracked tile, it will break.
If you push crates into holes, you can walk over them.
